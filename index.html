Quick guide

Saving something

Tap + Save, paste a link, and you're done — title and platform are detected automatically. Notes and a thumbnail are optional. With sync turned on, you only paste the link: Google Gemini (through our server) fills in the title and topics when you save. If the daily limit is reached, the form lets you enter the details yourself.

Saving straight from Instagram or TikTok

Install Sortagram (browser menu → Install app / Add to Home screen). On Android it then appears in the Share menu of Instagram, TikTok and your browser. You can also open sortagram.vercel.app/?url= followed by a link to pre-fill the Save form.

Organizing

Tag items with topics (like Food or Travel) and creators (like @someone) — up to you which, or both. Switch between viewing them together or separately using the row above your topic list.

Finding things again

Use search, or the Sort and Select buttons above your items — sort by date, name, or platform, star favorites to keep them on top, or select several items at once to delete or retag together.

Bringing in existing saves

Already have posts saved elsewhere? Use Import saved posts in the menu (⋮) with a file exported from Instagram/TikTok, and review what gets added before confirming.

Using it on more than one device

Sync across devices in the menu gives you a short code — no account or email. Enter that same code on another device to link it.

Your data

Everything lives on your own device unless you turn on sync. Save a backup file anytime from the menu, just in case.

Import items

Parsed entirely on your device — the file never leaves your browser.
